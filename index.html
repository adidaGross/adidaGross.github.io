01 · Test automation

Does your automation report look like this?

Twenty-three red lines. Which ones are real bugs in the product? Which are broken tests? Someone will spend the morning finding out, and tomorrow there will be new ones.

CI · e2e-tests · run #1482

What changed: every failure now has an owner and a next step. Real product bugs go to the developers with the evidence and a suggested fix. Broken tests are repaired automatically and re-run, so a person only reviews the fix.

These are the real results from my AI QA Lab demo project: 23 of 23 failures classified correctly against an answer key, and 8 of 8 broken tests repaired and passing. Ticket numbers are illustrative.
