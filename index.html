Why I do this

QA sits in the middle of everything: between developers and support, between what the plan says and what the product does. From there you see where the tools get in the way. Tickets nobody owns, the same bug reported three times, a test report that tells you something is red but not why. Most of my work is removing that friction, and my QA background means I test what I build for edge cases and for whether people actually adopt it.
