About Automation with Faultwise Consulting

Does your Jira look like this?

Everything is Highest. Nobody knows who owns what. The same bug is reported three times. Support creates users by hand, and the answers live in one person's head.

I've been on the inside of that. At my last company there were no defined workflows, no automations and no notifications. Customers asked support for basic things, like creating a user, and every ticket was investigated by hand. I set up Jira Service Management from scratch, rebuilt the R&D project with one board and clear workflows, wrote knowledge bases in Confluence, and built two AI agents with Rovo and Claude.

What I can set up for you

  • Jira and Confluence designed around how your team really works: workflows, boards, dashboards and automations, or a cleanup of an instance that grew out of control.
  • Jira Service Management: request types, queues, SLAs, a customer portal and escalation flows, so support stops being an inbox.
  • AI agents with Rovo and Claude that answer from your own knowledge base, triage tickets and find related bugs, connected through MCP where it helps.
  • Knowledge bases for R&D, support and customers, each open to the right people.
  • A move from waterfall to agile, with release planning, clear ownership and hands-on training for R&D, QA, support and services.

I also set up and migrate Linear and monday.com.

How I work

I start by sitting with the people who use the system every day, not only the managers who bought it. Then I map how work really moves, build the smallest setup that removes the friction, and train the team on it. I stay until it's used, because a workflow nobody follows is just another board.
